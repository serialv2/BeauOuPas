Child Safety Standards

BeauOuPas Application

Français · English

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Zero-tolerance commitment

BeauOuPas enforces a zero-tolerance policy regarding any child sexual abuse or exploitation content (CSAE/CSAM) or any behavior endangering the safety of a minor on our platform.

Our application does not allow and has never allowed:

  • Sharing, uploading or distributing child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
  • Solicitation, grooming or exploitation of minors
  • Any sexual or suggestive representation of minors
  • Unwanted contact between adults and minors
⚠️ Any content reported as containing or depicting a minor in a sexual context will be immediately removed, the relevant account will be permanently banned, and competent authorities will be notified where applicable (NCMEC in the United States, Pharos in France, INHOPE internationally).

2. Age restriction

BeauOuPas is intended exclusively for users aged 18 or older. This age restriction is:

  • Indicated on the Google Play Store listing
  • Configured in content rating (target audience: 18+)
  • Mentioned in our terms of service and privacy policy

At sign-up, users must confirm they are over 18. Accounts identified as belonging to minors are immediately suspended and deleted.

3. In-app reporting mechanism

Any BeauOuPas user can report inappropriate content, suspicious behavior, or an account presumed to belong to a minor, directly from the application:

  1. Tap on the relevant account or content
  2. Select the "Report" option in the menu
  3. Choose the category (minor, inappropriate content, harassment, etc.)
  4. Add a comment if necessary and submit

Every report is reviewed within 48 hours by our team. Reports related to a minor's safety are treated as absolute priority and reviewed as soon as possible (typically within 24 hours).

5. Actions and legal compliance

BeauOuPas commits to complying with all applicable laws and regulations regarding the protection of minors, including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR - EU)
  • French Criminal Code (articles 227-22 et seq.)
  • Digital Services Act (DSA - EU)
  • International Google Play standards regarding child safety

5.1 Procedure in case of CSAM content discovery

  1. Immediate removal of content and banning of the relevant account
  2. Reporting to competent authorities:
    • In France: PHAROS (internet-signalement.gouv.fr)
    • In the United States: NCMEC CyberTipline
    • Internationally: INHOPE
  3. Preservation of evidence in accordance with legal requirements
  4. Full cooperation with investigating authorities

6. Moderation and prevention

Several mechanisms are in place to prevent abuse:

  • Reactive moderation: human review of all reports
  • Account banning: ability to permanently block a user
  • Strict age restriction at sign-up (18+)
  • Identity verification via email address upon account creation
  • Visible and enforced zero-tolerance policy
