Account Deletion

BeauOuPas Application

Français · English

Last updated: May 25, 2026

1. Recommended method: delete directly in the app

The fastest and simplest way to delete your BeauOuPas account is to do it directly from the app:

  1. Open the BeauOuPas app on your phone
  2. Log in to your account if you are not already logged in
  3. Go to Profile (icon at the bottom right)
  4. Tap Settings
  5. Scroll down to "Delete my account"
  6. Confirm the deletion (you will be asked to confirm twice)
⚠️ Deletion is immediate, permanent and irreversible. All data associated with your account will be erased.

2. Alternative method: by email

If you no longer have access to the app (lost phone, uninstalled app, etc.), you can request account deletion by email.

Procedure

  1. Send an email to steenkiste.ai@gmail.com
  2. Subject: BeauOuPas account deletion
  3. Include in the message:
    • The email address associated with your account
    • Your username (if you remember it)
  4. The email must be sent from the email address associated with the account (to verify your identity)

📧 Request deletion by email

Your request will be processed within 30 days maximum. You will receive an email confirmation once the deletion is complete.

3. Data that will be deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is erased immediately and irreversibly:

  • Your account (email address, hashed password, identifiers)
  • Your profile (username, profile picture, biography)
  • All selfies taken during games
  • Your votes, quiz answers, created games
  • Your friendships and invitations
  • Your created series and projects
  • Your private messages and chat history
  • Your preferences and settings

4. Data that may be retained

For legal or technical reasons, some data may be retained for a limited period after your account deletion:

  • Anonymized technical logs: up to 90 days (for diagnostic and security purposes), without any personal identifier
  • Data shared with third parties (Google AdMob): subject to those third parties' retention policies — see our privacy policy

No personally identifiable data is retained beyond these periods.
